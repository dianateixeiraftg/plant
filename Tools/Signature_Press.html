Printing and folding

  1. Print the exported PDF double-sided at actual size, with scaling turned off.
  2. Print a single signature first and check that the back of each sheet lines up with the front.
  3. Fold every sheet in half along the dashed line and crease it with a bone folder.
  4. Nest the sheets of one signature, with sheet 1 on the outside.
  5. Stack the signatures in order. The spine marks form a staircase when the order is right.
  6. Sew or glue the spine, then trim the three open edges.
