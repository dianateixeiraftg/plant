Notifications

Reminders are checked every 30 seconds while this page is open (a pinned tab works well). Anything that came due while it was closed is sent the next time you open it.

Browser

Browsers only allow notifications on secure pages (https, such as GitHub Pages, or localhost). On iPhone, add the page to the Home Screen first.

Email with EmailJS

In EmailJS, create an email service and a template. Set the template’s “To email” to {{to_email}}, the subject to {{subject}}, and use {{title}}, {{message}}, {{deadline}} and {{notes}} in the body. Pictures are not included in the email.
